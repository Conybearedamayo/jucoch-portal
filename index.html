Zero-Identity & Masked Privacy

Absolute Confidentiality

Dual-layer privacy architecture. User emails are masked (e.g. j***z@gmail.com) and sensitive journal reflections undergo automatic character masking (e.g. 🔒 Fi***l Ex***m), ensuring zero identity exposure even to database administrators.

Admin DB View: ENCRYPTED & MASKED
🔒 "j***z@school.edu: Stressed about Fi***l Ex***m"
📊

Dynamic Wellness Scoring

Holistic Composite Index

Mathematical wellness scoring algorithm integrating 3 daily physiological and emotional signals: mood check-in sentiment, verified sleep duration logs, and mindful activity minutes to formulate a daily resilience score (0-100).

😊 Mood Sentiment 🌙 Sleep Rhythm Logs 🏃 Mindful Activity Tracking

10 Mindfulness Avatars

Gamified Anonymity

Students express their identity safely through 10 collectible nature & animal avatars designed to foster emotional psychological safety without real-name or profile photo requirements.
